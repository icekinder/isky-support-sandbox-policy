# Isky Support Sandbox data deletion

Effective 27 September 2026.

Email **inekkazlr@gmail.com** with the subject **Isky Support Sandbox privacy request** to request access to or removal of local records from the one-person WhatsApp sandbox test. Include the WhatsApp number used in the test and the approximate test date. Do not send passwords, payment details, or identity documents.

The operator will verify the request through the existing test contact. The local check covers the inbox, held replies, booking-request records, review records, diagnostic logs, test exports, and other local copies. The operator will acknowledge requests within two working days and complete verified local removal within seven calendar days after verifying the request. This is not an automatic deletion service.

The receiver cannot erase records held by Meta or the temporary HTTPS tunnel provider. Contact those providers through their own processes for their copies.
