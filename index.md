# Isky Support Sandbox privacy notice

Effective 27 September 2026.

Isky Support Sandbox is a one-person WhatsApp development test operated by Iskander Nekkaz - Le Roux. It is separate from MariDeal customer support. Only the invited test participant should message the Meta-provided test number. Please use invented questions and booking details. Do not send real booking, payment, identity, health, or other confidential information.

## What we process

For the invited sender, the local receiver records the WhatsApp sender number, message identifier, time, text, and receiving account and phone identifiers. It can also retain a held reply, a booking-request state, or a record that needs human review. These records let the operator inspect the test and avoid processing the same message twice. The receiver does not download attachments. Correctly formed signed messages from other senders are acknowledged without storing or drafting their content. Invalid events may be rejected.

Meta delivers webhook traffic through a temporary localhost.run HTTPS tunnel to a local receiver during the test window. Meta and localhost.run may process traffic under their own terms. The local receiver does not control their copies.

AI drafting and Freshdesk tickets are disabled for this test. The receiver does not automatically send WhatsApp replies, make bookings, take payments, or change a MariDeal account. Any later use of AI, Freshdesk, or a MariDeal number needs an updated notice and a separate decision.

## How long we keep local records

The operator will remove identifiable local records after the held review is checked, within seven calendar days after the test message arrives. This is a manual operator task. The receiver has no automatic deletion timer. The local review will cover the inbox, held replies, booking-request records, diagnostic logs, test exports, and any other local copies.

## Contact and requests

For privacy questions or a request to access or remove local test records, email **inekkazlr@gmail.com** with the subject **Isky Support Sandbox privacy request**. The operator will acknowledge requests within two working days and complete verified local removal within seven calendar days after verifying the request. [How to request deletion](data-deletion.html) explains what to include.
